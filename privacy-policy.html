Privacy Policy

for: HomeMediStock

This Privacy Policy has been effective from December 04, 2025.

Please read the Privacy Policy before using the application.

Data Collection

HomeMediStock is designed with your privacy in mind. All of your medication data (names, quantities, doses, expiry dates, descriptions, photos, leaflets, and reminders) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal health information to our servers.

Automatic Data Collection

When you use the application, it may automatically collect limited technical data. This data may include:

  • IP Address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Language settings

This data is only used to support app functionality (e.g., localizing language) and is not stored or shared by us.

OCR Processing (Optional)

Premium users (and free users with limits) can use OCR to extract medicine details from photos. If enabled in settings, OCR-extracted data or photos may be processed by third-party AI services. Users can toggle this feature off in settings to keep all processing local. No health data is stored by third-party services.

How We Use Your Data

Your medication data never leaves your device except when optional OCR processing is enabled. We use it only for medication tracking, expiry notifications, dosage reminders, and optional AI-assisted medicine entry.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties except when optional OCR processing is enabled (user-controlled via settings). All other features like photo uploads (max 5 per medicine), leaflet pasting, and reminders operate locally.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely on your device using Android's built-in data protection mechanisms. We recommend using your device's screen lock feature for additional security.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data. You can export, delete, or modify your medication entries at any time through the app's interface. You can also disable OCR processing in settings. If you uninstall the app, all data will be removed unless you have created an external backup.

Third-Party Services

Adapty: We use Adapty to manage in-app subscriptions, premium features, and limit increases. Adapty may collect anonymized technical and purchase-related data to process subscriptions. Adapty does not access your medication data.

Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect anonymized data for ad personalization but does not access your medication data.

Third-Party AI (Optional OCR): When OCR is enabled in settings, photos or extracted data may be sent to third-party AI services for processing medicine details. This can be disabled anytime. These services do not store your data.

Permissions Required

To function properly, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet connection – Required for subscriptions via Adapty, ads via AdMob, and optional OCR processing.
  • Camera – Needed for OCR photo scanning to auto-extract medicine details.
  • Storage – For saving up to 5 photos per medicine entry locally.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to change this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon being posted on this page. Please review this page periodically for updates.
